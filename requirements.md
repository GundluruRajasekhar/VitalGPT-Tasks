# Requirements
## Problem requirements
- Unify health data, reminders, risk alerts and report explanations in one platform.
## Functional
- FR1 Dashboard with health overview, AI insights, risk scores
- FR2 Risk Detection agent: predictive alerts, chronic disease monitoring
- FR3 Medication reminders and adherence tracking
- FR4 Medical report analysis with plain-language output
- FR5 Nutrition, Fitness, Appointment, Mind Wellness agents
- FR6 One-click emergency (SOS) assistance
## AI (Gemini)
- Gemini API: report analysis, assistant, recommendations
- Gemini Live: voice interaction
- Gemma: edge/offline, privacy-focused processing
## Market
- Primary: India, US, UK. Secondary: Canada, Australia, UAE, Singapore.
- Segments: individuals, families, elderly, chronic patients, providers.
## Non-functional
- Security (JWT, OAuth, RBAC, encrypted records, audit logs), WCAG accessibility, responsive dark/light UI, PWA/offline.
